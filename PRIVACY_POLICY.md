# SAYARTAY 2D PRO — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 11 October 2026**  
**Developer: SAYARTAY 2D PRO**  
**Android package: com.sayartay.twodpro.app**  
**Contact: [soetay1@gmail.com](mailto:soetay1@gmail.com)**

This policy explains how SAYARTAY 2D PRO handles information when you use its Android app and connected online service. The app displays 2D results and provides historical and statistical analysis. It requires an internet connection.

## Information processed

- **Trial and activation:** You may enter a display name and a phone number or User ID. The service processes this information to identify your trial or licence. The licence database stores a SHA-256 hash of the normalized phone number/User ID, the display name, trial and licence dates, status, and related timestamps. Activation passwords are stored as salted password hashes.
- **Persistent access:** The service uses an encrypted browser/WebView cookie containing an opaque access token so you can return while your licence is active. A hash of this token is stored on the server. The access cookie does not contain your phone number/User ID or activation password.
- **Data you choose to provide:** CSV/Excel files you upload are sent to the online service for parsing and analysis. History records saved through database features are stored in the connected history database and may be available to other app users as part of the shared results history. Do not upload personal or confidential information in history files.
- **Technical information:** Internet requests disclose information such as your IP address and request/device/browser details to the services receiving them. Hosting and service providers may process connection, security and diagnostic logs.
- **Support:** If you contact us by email or through the app's external contact link, we receive the information you choose to send.

## How information is used

Information is used to provide results and analysis, manage the one-time trial and activation, maintain access sessions, operate the history database, check for app updates, resolve support requests, and protect the service from misuse.

## Service providers and external links

The connected service uses Streamlit Community Cloud for hosting and Supabase for database services. The Android app checks GitHub for public release information; update downloads and external contact links open a browser or another app. These services receive the requests sent to them and operate under their own privacy policies. Data may be processed outside your country.

- [Streamlit / Snowflake privacy notice](https://www.snowflake.com/en/legal/privacy/privacy-policy/)
- [Supabase privacy policy](https://supabase.com/privacy)
- [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

Personal trial/activation records are not intentionally published in the public download repository. We do not sell personal trial or activation information. Support messages sent through external messaging services are also subject to those services' policies.

## Device permissions and local storage

The current Android app requests internet permission. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, SMS or location permissions. Its WebView stores cookies and web storage needed for the connected service. Installing an APK update requires your confirmation through Android; the app does not silently install updates.

## Retention and security

Licence records are retained while needed to operate trials and licences, prevent repeat trials or abuse, and resolve support matters. Shared history records remain part of the results database until corrected or removed through the service's authorized processes. Hosting providers may have separate log and backup retention practices.

The service uses HTTPS connections, password/token hashing, encrypted access cookies and server-side access controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices and requests

You can stop using the app, clear its app data/cookies, or uninstall it. Clearing local data or uninstalling does not automatically delete server-side licence or history records and does not reset a previously used trial.

For access, correction or deletion requests concerning your personal information, contact **soetay1@gmail.com**. We may need to verify that the request belongs to you. Some records may need to be retained for security, trial-abuse prevention or other applicable requirements. Do not email passwords, signing keys or secret tokens.

## Children

The service is not intended for children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us to request review and deletion.

## Policy updates

We may revise this policy as the app or its connected services change. The effective date above will identify the current version.
